## Customer job

Help an insurance shopper understand the immediate useful step, disclose only needed facts, and make a **deliberate, permissioned transition to a licensed human advisor**. Help the agency receive enough evidence to respond intelligently with less redundant effort.

The success measure is **a consented, received handoff that becomes a genuine useful conversation**, later linked only through governed identified evidence to quote/bind outcomes. Pageviews, anonymous questions answered and scores are not leads, contact permission or binds.

## The operating boundary

408FARMERS owns acquisition, anonymous signal UI, channel choice and explicit consent. CoverageFit owns evidence interpretation, Opportunity Priority/FIV, NBA, persistent agency context and outcome measurement. AgencyZoom retains its cadence/CRM role; real SMS remains separately reviewed. Insurance satellite sites remain acquisition entrypoints, not independent intelligence businesses.
